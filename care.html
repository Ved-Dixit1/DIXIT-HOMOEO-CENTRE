Care begins
with a conversation.

The clinic listing mentions these areas. This page shares listing information only; contact Dr. Dixit to confirm current services and whether a visit is right for you.

01

Kidney stones

Ask the clinic about evaluation and care options. Sudden severe pain, fever, vomiting or difficulty passing urine needs urgent medical assessment.

02

Thyroid disorders

Discuss your symptoms, test results and current treatment with a qualified clinician. Do not stop prescribed thyroid medicine without medical guidance.

03

Viral fever

Contact a doctor for persistent or worsening fever. Seek urgent care for breathing difficulty, confusion, dehydration or other severe symptoms.

04

Diabetes management

Continue your prescribed diabetes care and monitoring. Ask your physician before changing medicines or adding complementary treatments.

Homoeopathy is a complementary system of medicine. Evidence for its effectiveness is limited, and it should not replace treatments supported by established medical evidence. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or go to a hospital.
